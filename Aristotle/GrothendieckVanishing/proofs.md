The live main theorem gap is:

The closed-subset choice and pushforward term are already proved. What remains is

## Lemma 1: Morphisms out of `zeroOutsideInt` are determined by the generator

Suggested statement:

```lean
/-- A morphism out of `zeroOutsideInt U` is determined by the image of the
distinguished generator on `U`. -/
theorem zeroOutsideInt_hom_ext_from_generator
    {X : TopCat.{u}} {U : Opens X}
    {F : TopCat.Sheaf AddCommGrpCat.{u} X}
    (f g : TopCat.Sheaf.zeroOutsideInt U ⟶ F)
    (h :
      f.val.app (op U) (TopCat.Sheaf.zeroOutsideInt.generator U) =
      g.val.app (op U) (TopCat.Sheaf.zeroOutsideInt.generator U)) :
    f = g
```

Natural-language proof:

- First prove the analogous presheaf statement for `Presheaf.constZ.zeroOutside U`.
- For any open `W ≤ U`, every section of `(constZ.zeroOutside U)(W)` is an
  integer multiple of the restriction of the distinguished generator on `U`.
- Naturality implies that a presheaf morphism out of `constZ.zeroOutside U` is
  completely determined by its value on the generator over `U`.
- Pass to sheaves by precomposing with `toSheafify`.
- Since `zeroOutsideInt U` is the sheafification of `constZ.zeroOutside U`,
  agreement on the generator implies equality of sheaf morphisms.

This is the clean universality theorem behind `zeroOutsideInt.sHom`.

## Lemma 2: Single-generator case

Suggested statement:

```lean
/-- Any sheaf generated by a single section is an epi image of `zeroOutsideInt U`,
so vanishing for epi images of `zeroOutsideInt` implies vanishing for singly
generated sheaves. -/
theorem subsingleton_of_singleGeneratedSheaf_of_zeroOutside
    {X : TopCat.{u}} {U : Opens X}
    {F : TopCat.Sheaf AddCommGrpCat.{u} X}
    (n : ℕ)
    (hzero : ∀ {G : TopCat.Sheaf AddCommGrpCat.{u} X} {V : Opens X}
      (f : TopCat.Sheaf.zeroOutsideInt V ⟶ G), Epi f →
      Subsingleton (Sheaf.H G n))
    (s : F.presheaf.obj (op U)) :
    Subsingleton (Sheaf.H (TopCat.Sheaf.singleGeneratedSheaf s) n)
```

Natural-language proof:

- By definition, `singleGeneratedSheaf s` is the image of
  `singleGeneratorMap s : zeroOutsideInt U ⟶ F`.
- The factorization
  `zeroOutsideInt U ⟶ image(singleGeneratorMap s)`
  is always epi.
- Therefore `singleGeneratedSheaf s` is an epi image of `zeroOutsideInt U`.
- Apply `hzero`.

This lemma is already present in `FiniteGeneratorReduction.lean`.

## Lemma 3: Finite-generator induction

Suggested statement:

```lean
/-- If every epi image of every `zeroOutsideInt V` has vanishing in degree `n`,
then every finitely generated subsheaf has vanishing in degree `n`. -/
theorem subsingleton_of_finsetGeneratedSheaf_of_zeroOutside
    {X : TopCat.{u}} {F : TopCat.Sheaf AddCommGrpCat.{u} X}
    (n : ℕ)
    (hzero : ∀ {G : TopCat.Sheaf AddCommGrpCat.{u} X} {V : Opens X}
      (f : TopCat.Sheaf.zeroOutsideInt V ⟶ G), Epi f →
      Subsingleton (Sheaf.H G n))
    (S : Finset (TopCat.Sheaf.SectionIndex F))
    [HasCoproduct fun σ : {σ // σ ∈ S} => TopCat.Sheaf.zeroOutsideInt σ.1.1] :
    Subsingleton (Sheaf.H (TopCat.Sheaf.finsetGeneratedSheaf S) n)
```

Natural-language proof:

- Induct on the finite set `S`.
- Base case `S = ∅`:
  the generator map is the zero map from the initial coproduct, so its image is
  the zero sheaf. Hence its cohomology is zero.
- Inductive step:
  write `S = insert σ S'`.
- Let `F'` be the subsheaf generated by `S'`.
- Let `F''` be the subsheaf generated by `insert σ S'`.
- The existing short exact sequence built from
  `oldGeneratedToAdjoinGenerated` shows that `F'' / F'` is generated by the one
  new section `σ`.
- By the induction hypothesis, `H^n(F') = 0`.
- By Lemma 2, `H^n(F'' / F') = 0`.
- Apply the long exact sequence in the middle term to get `H^n(F'') = 0`.

This is the missing global Step 3B theorem. The one-step adjoining machinery is
already formalized in `FiniteGeneratorReduction.lean`.

## Lemma 4: Filtered colimit reduction

Suggested statement:

```lean
/-- If every finitely generated subsheaf of `F` has vanishing in degree `n`,
then `F` itself has vanishing in degree `n`. -/
theorem subsingleton_of_all_finsetGenerated
    {X : TopCat.{u}} [NoetherianSpace X]
    (n : ℕ)
    (F : TopCat.Sheaf AddCommGrpCat.{u} X)
    (hfin : ∀ (S : Finset (TopCat.Sheaf.SectionIndex F))
      [HasCoproduct fun σ : {σ // σ ∈ S} => TopCat.Sheaf.zeroOutsideInt σ.1.1],
      Subsingleton (Sheaf.H (TopCat.Sheaf.finsetGeneratedSheaf S) n)) :
    Subsingleton (Sheaf.H F n)
```

Natural-language proof:

- Let `F_S` be the subsheaf generated by a finite set `S` of local sections.
- These form a directed system under inclusion.
- Every local section of `F` lies in some singleton-generated subsheaf, hence in
  some `F_S`.
- Therefore `F` is the filtered colimit of the `F_S`.
- Use Hartshorne Proposition 2.9, i.e. cohomology commutes with filtered
  colimits on noetherian spaces, to identify `H^n(F)` with the filtered colimit
  of the `H^n(F_S)`.
- Since each `H^n(F_S)` is a subsingleton, so is the colimit.

This is the missing Step 3A theorem.

## Lemma 5: Epi images of `zeroOutsideInt` vanish

Suggested statement:

```lean
/-- On an irreducible noetherian space, every epi image of `zeroOutsideInt U`
has vanishing in degrees above the Krull dimension. -/
theorem subsingleton_of_epi_image_zeroOutside
    (X : TopCat.{u}) [NoetherianSpace X] [IrreducibleSpace X]
    (n : ℕ) (hn : n > topologicalKrullDim X)
    (U : Opens X)
    {G : TopCat.Sheaf AddCommGrpCat.{u} X}
    (f : TopCat.Sheaf.zeroOutsideInt U ⟶ G) (hf : Epi f) :
    Subsingleton (Sheaf.H G n)
```

Natural-language proof:

- Let `R := kernel f`.
- Because `f` is epi, there is a short exact sequence
  `0 → R → zeroOutsideInt U → G → 0`.
- Step 5 already proves vanishing for `zeroOutsideInt U` itself.
- To prove vanishing for `R`, use Hartshorne Step 4:
  every nonzero subsheaf of `zeroOutsideInt U` can, after shrinking to a
  nonempty open `V ⊆ U`, be reduced by a short exact sequence to
  `zeroOutsideInt V` plus a quotient supported on a proper closed subset.
- The quotient vanishes by `PushforwardHVanishing` and the induction hypothesis
  on the smaller closed subset.
- Therefore `R` has vanishing as well.
- Apply the long exact sequence to conclude vanishing for `G`.

This packages Hartshorne Steps 4 and 5 into the form needed by Lemmas 2 and 3.
